Shah Rukh Khan death threat: Mumbai Police arrests Chhattisgarh lawyer for threatening SRK

Shah Rukh Khan death threat: The Bollywood actor received a death threat earlier. (Image: Instagram) Shah Rukh Khan: The Mumbai Police of Tuesday arrested a lawyers in Chhattisgarh’s Raipur for issuing a death threat to Bollywood superstar Shah Rukh Khan. The lawyer, identified as Faizan Khan, allegedly threatened Shah Rukh Khan last week and demanded a ransom of Rs 50 lakh.

A senior Raipur police officer confirmed the arrest of Faizan Khan, 42, and said he would be produced before a local court in the city by the Mumbai police seeking his transit remand. Faizan was summoned by the Mumbai police in connection with the threat call to Shah Rukh Khan last Tuesday, but he failed to appear before them, Indian Express reported.
